About

#about

I'm a Help Desk Technician at West Coast Magnetics, a magnetics manufacturer in Stockton, CA. Day to day I handle endpoint deployment and management, end-user support, network infrastructure, and support for the test-lab instruments that production and engineering depend on — across the Stockton headquarters and a second manufacturing site in Mexico that I support remotely. That's 200+ computers and 100+ users in total — and the hands-on side of it too: replacing failed hardware, keeping label printers, barcode scanners, and floor displays working, and sorting out DHCP, DNS, and static-IP problems at the desk.

My strong lean is toward automating anything that can be automated. Most of my work ships as PowerShell: remoting tools, PDQ Deploy packages, scheduled-task installers with verification scripts, and repair utilities. When a problem is deeper — a recurring BSOD, a driver a Windows update keeps breaking, an instrument that won't talk over GPIB — I dig into event logs, minidumps, and the driver store until the root cause is found and documented.

Off the clock I build web apps I actually use — a task tracker, a notes app that syncs across devices, a phone-first money tracker — and I'm working through Network+ and CCNA on the way to a network engineering role.
